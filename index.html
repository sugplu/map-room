Map Room

Every public map and plan database for a place, on one page. Made by Dominares.

Pick a state and a county. Map Room then asks the public map services for everything they hold on that place, newest information each time you open it.

Sources are public records services: ArcGIS Online, Socrata open data, the Library of Congress, and the US Census. Map Room does not copy them. It reads them live. Your pins, notes and changes stay in this browser until you save them to a file.
